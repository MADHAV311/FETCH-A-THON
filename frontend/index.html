About AI Adaptive Traffic Signal Digital Twin

College Engineering Demonstration Project.

1. The Core Idea

Conventional traffic signals use Fixed-Time Control: every approach receives a predefined green interval (e.g., 30s) regardless of whether cars are waiting. When traffic is uneven or congested, fixed-time signals waste green time on empty roads while queues pile up elsewhere.

The AI Adaptive Traffic Signal dynamically detects queue lengths and waiting delays, assigning green time proportionally to roads with the greatest need, while preserving fairness to prevent quiet roads from starving.

2. Priority Calculation

Priority(Road) = (Queue_Count × 1.2 × 2.5) + (Max_Wait_Time × 1.5) + (Total_Delay / 8 × 0.6) + (Arrival_Rate / 10 × 0.4) + Starvation_Bonus

3. Green Duration Calculation

Green_Duration = clamp(Min_Green, Startup_Lost_Time + (Queue_Per_Lane × Saturation_Headway), Max_Green)

If a queue clears before the maximum green duration expires (gap-out), the controller transitions to yellow early so other waiting approaches can be served sooner.
